Master's & Bachelor's Thesis Supervision

Across his career, Prof. Martalò has supervised (as advisor or co-advisor) approximately two dozen Master's-level theses and a total of 36 Bachelor's-level theses (25 as advisor, 11 as co-advisor) at the Universities of Cagliari and Parma. Recent Master's supervisees at Cagliari include G. Nurcis (sustainable energy data certification via Edge-IoT & blockchain, 2025), A. Mancosu (Edge-based IoT traffic fingerprinting, 2025), M. Flumini (encrypted traffic classification with CNNs, 2023) and G. Pettorru (secure low-latency IoT communications, 2022). The full list is available in the complete CV.
