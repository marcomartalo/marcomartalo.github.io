Research & Projects

Digital communication systems and networking, from theory to funded applied research

My research activity is in the general field of digital communication systems, with particular attention to wireless environments — from traditional point-to-point links to advanced multi-user and ad-hoc networking scenarios. Current contributions are organized around three active threads, building on an earlier career centered on distributed detection and transmission over non-ideal channels.

① Trustworthy & secure networking ② Advanced applications for communication networks (IoT) ③ Audio signal processing ④ Transmission & signal processing for sensor networks ⑤ Transmission systems on non-ideal channels
